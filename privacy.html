Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

WayVelle is a trip planner for travelling together. This page explains what we store, who can see it, and how to delete it. In short: we keep what's needed to plan and share your trips, we never sell it, show ads or track you across apps, and your Vault documents are encrypted so that even we can't open them.

Your account

You can use WayVelle without an account; your trips then stay on your iPhone. If you sign in with Apple, we receive your name (the first time only) and an email address, which may be Apple's private relay address. We use them to sign you in and to show members who changed a plan.

Your trips

When you're signed in, your trips, plans, stays, times, notes and the travellers you add are stored on our servers (Supabase, in Frankfurt, Germany) so they sync between devices and with the people you invite. Members of a trip see that trip; nobody else does. Trips you delete wait in Deleted Trips for 7 days, then are removed.

Files you attach to plans (tickets, confirmations) are stored with the trip and can be opened by its members.

The Vault

Documents in your Vault (passport, insurance, visas) are encrypted on your iPhone with AES-256 before they are uploaded. The key is kept only on your devices, synced by iCloud Keychain. Our servers store only encrypted data, including encrypted file names. If our servers were ever broken into, these files could not be opened, not by the attacker and not by us. If no device of yours has the key any more, the files can't be recovered by anyone.

Sharing

An invite link lets someone join one trip with the role you chose. "Send a copy" stores a copy of the trip, without attachments, travellers, members, passenger names or booking notes, that anyone with its link or code can add to their own WayVelle. Hotel names and addresses are included only if you leave "Include where we stayed" on.

Location

With your permission, WayVelle uses your location on your iPhone to show where you are on a travel day, to plan the way to your next stop, and to guide hikes. Your location is not stored on our servers, except where you choose to save it in a plan (for example, "Left the trail at" a stop), or while you share it.

Sharing your location. When your group splits up on a trip day, you can choose to share where you are with the people on that trip, for an hour or until a time you pick (at most 24 hours). While you share, your iPhone sends your current position about every 30 seconds while you move, also when WayVelle is in the background (iOS shows the location indicator). We keep only your latest position, never a history. Only members of that trip can see it. It stops at the time you chose or when you tap Stop, and your position is then deleted from our servers within minutes. Deleting your account deletes it at once.

Alerts

If you allow notifications, we store your device's push token and, on travel days, the trains or buses and flights you're following, so we can alert you about delays, platform and gate changes. These records are deleted within days.

Services we use

  • Supabase (Frankfurt) hosts accounts, trips and files.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications, maps and weather.
  • Timetables and weather: opentransportdata.swiss (journey searches between stops), MeteoSwiss, swisstopo (hiking trails). These receive places and times, never your identity.
  • Flight status: our server asks a flight-data provider about flight numbers and dates only.
  • Photos of places come from Wikimedia Commons.
  • Google Maps or Waze are opened only when you choose them for directions; their own privacy policies apply there.

Deleting your data

In WayVelle, go to Settings → your name → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, the trips you own (for everyone on them), your Vault and your alert records. Trips other people own stay with them. Signing out instead removes your trips from that iPhone but keeps them in your account.

Children

WayVelle isn't directed at children under 13. You can add children as travellers on a trip (name, age and needs you enter); they don't have accounts.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Questions or requests: privacy@wayvelle.com.
